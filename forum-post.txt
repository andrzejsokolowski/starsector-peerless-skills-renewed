[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
A third level past Elite for your fourteen combat skills: Peerless.
You buy it with Peerless skill points on the Second-in-Command Character screen (C).

[color=red]Requires LunaLib, LazyLib, PatchLib and Second-in-Command.[/color]
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Peerless Skill Points[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]The Character screen shows "Peerless skill points" under your level.[/li]
[li]Click an Elite combat skill, then Save: it becomes Peerless and gets a gold diamond. Each costs one point.[/li]
[li]Click a Peerless skill to undo it and get the point back.[/li]
[li]Every combat skill tooltip has a Peerless part after Elite.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Where Points Come From[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]With Achievements - Renewed: big achievements pay a point each, once per campaign. A campaign that does everything earns enough for all fourteen skills; a normal one earns 1 to 4.[/li]
[li]Without it, or if you choose: every few full experience bars past the level cap make a point.[/li]
[li]A LunaLib setting speeds up or slows down how fast points come.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Effects[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]Helmsmanship:[/b] accelerating backwards is twice as fast.[/li]
[li][b]Combat Endurance:[/b] once per battle, at 10% hull the hull cannot drop further for 5 seconds.[/li]
[li][b]Impact Mitigation:[/b] every projectile or missile that hits your armor or hull deals 20 less damage, before armor reduction.[/li]
[li][b]Damage Control:[/b] engines and weapons repair faster the higher your combat readiness.[/li]
[li][b]Field Modulation:[/b] the first overload each battle is cancelled and the ship vents, with +200% maneuverability fading over 5 seconds.[/li]
[li][b]Target Analysis:[/b] every enemy ship starts with a weak spot on its outer edge: 1 to 4 armor cells (frigate to capital) with no armor.[/li]
[li][b]Systems Expertise:[/b] using the ship system gives +50% top speed and maneuverability, fading over 5 seconds.[/li]
[li][b]Point Defense:[/b] PD hits on missiles have a 10% chance to disarm the warhead.[/li]
[li][b]Energy Weapon Mastery:[/b] energy weapons deal 25% more damage to armor.[/li]
[li][b]Ballistic Mastery:[/b] while firing, weapons build up to perfect autofire accuracy and no recoil over 5 seconds.[/li]
[li][b]Gunnery Implants:[/b] weapons with less range than your longest-range one gain 10% of its base range.[/li]
[li][b]Ordnance Expertise:[/b] weapons cost 1 / 2 / 4 fewer ordnance points (small / medium / large).[/li]
[li][b]Polarized Armor:[/b] after 3 seconds without taking damage, armor rebuilds to half of each cell's full value, only once per cell per battle.[/li]
[li][b]Missile Specialization:[/b] guided missiles turn 50% faster; unguided ones slowly steer toward a target.[/li]
[li]A LunaLib setting switches to the Original effects.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Officers[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Optional, off by default. Turn it on in LunaLib and pick the cost: Peerless points, story points or credits.[/li]
[li]An Officers button on the Character screen lists your officers and their Elite combat skills. Click one to make it Peerless; click again to undo it and get back what it cost.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Console Commands[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]PeerlessAddPoints[/b] [amount], [b]PeerlessStatus[/b], [b]PeerlessUnlockAll[/b], [b]PeerlessRefund[/b][/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Safe to add to a game in progress. Do not remove it from one.[/li]
[li]Do not use it together with the first Peerless Skills mod.[/li]
[/list]
