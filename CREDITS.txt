Peerless Skills - Renewed

- Peerless skills come from Starsector itself (Fractal Softworks): the game had them before 0.95.
- Peerless Skills by c4tntnitro-netizen (MIT) brought them back as a mod; this mod rebuilt the fourteen
  Original effects from that mod's descriptions. No code was copied.
- Second-in-Command and PatchLib by Lukas04: this mod draws its Peerless points, icons and tooltip
  section on Second-in-Command's Character screen through PatchLib hooks, without changing either mod.
